THE NOTCHOne notch. Every cloud.

Each nimbi app keeps its own island at the notch. Swipe it sideways with two fingers and the notch moves to the next app you have open, which opens with its page drifting in. Relay, Skyline, Tempo, in the order you installed them.

THE SECOND CLOUD OUTSkyline

The weather in your menu bar: the cloud and the temperature, with drizzle under it when rain starts within the hour. Click for the sky now, the next 24 hours as a skyline of rain bars under the temperature, and the week ahead. The cloud wears the weather: a sun behind it, rain falling past it, a moon and stars at night.

THE NEWEST CLOUDTempo

A focus timer in the menu bar. Tempo is Italian for both time and weather. While you work, the cloud becomes an hourglass and its sand runs down. When time is up it turns into a ringing bell, on a break a steaming mug, and on a long break a sleepy moon. If you like, soft rain plays while you focus.
